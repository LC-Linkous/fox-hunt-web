fox-hunt-web · Last updated 2026-10-20 · Works in Chrome and Edge via Web Serial; audio decoding works in any browser. Page GPL-2.0, firmware GPL-3.0. Maintained by LC Linkous. For the Ham Radio Village DC34 fox; not affiliated with the Village, NiceRF, or Seeed. No license is needed to listen; one is needed to transmit, and the firmware ships with transmit disabled. Firmware: the fox needs code.py (firmware 0.3.1) saved to it once with Thonny — how. Also on the latest release.
